Focus New tab extension for Chrome

A calmer new tab for the sites you actually use.

Focus is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with three spaces: Focus for a small set of daily sites, Focus+ for a larger grid with folders, and Cloud for notes, images and drawings.

Install from Chrome Web Store Privacy Policy Terms of Service GitHub

What Focus does

Focus and Focus+

Keep a short list of daily sites in Focus and a larger, folder-based grid in Focus+. Drag icons into folders, reorder them, and keep the layout across screen sizes.

Cloud board

Place images, notes and freehand drawings on a shared board. The board uses a fixed layout canvas so it looks consistent on different monitors.

Themes

Choose color themes or image themes. Background images, accent colors and icon treatments are applied locally in your browser.

Optional Google Drive backups

If you choose to create a backup, Focus stores a ZIP archive in the hidden appDataFolder of your own Google Drive so you can restore your settings on another device.

Google Drive backups

Backups are optional and start only when you press “Create a new backup” in Focus settings. Focus asks for a Google OAuth token with the drive.appdata scope and uploads a ZIP archive to the app-specific folder in your own Google Drive. The archive contains your Focus settings, links, folders, background images and Cloud board images.

Focus does not read, edit or delete any other Google Drive files. The developer has no access to your Drive or to the backup archive. You can delete backups in Focus settings or revoke access in your Google Account permissions at any time.

Google API compliance

Focus complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Focus does not use Google APIs, including the Google Drive API, to create, generate or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCI).

Privacy and permissions

Focus has no analytics, no advertising and no developer servers. Your links, themes, backgrounds and Cloud board data stay in your browser. The extension uses network access for favicons, search queries you submit, and optional Google Drive backups. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of permissions and data handling.

Optional access to open tabs is requested only when you choose “Open tabs”; URLs and titles are saved locally and are not sent to the developer.
