Focus New tab extension for Chrome

Focus — a calmer new tab for the sites you actually use.

Focus is a Chrome extension that replaces the new tab page with three spaces: Focus for a small set of daily sites, Focus+ for a larger grid with folders, and Cloud for notes, images and drawings.

Install from Chrome Web Store Privacy Policy Terms of Service GitHub

What Focus does

Focus and Focus+

Keep a short list of daily sites in Focus and a larger, folder-based grid in Focus+. Drag icons into folders, reorder them, and keep the layout across screen sizes.

Cloud board

Place images, notes and freehand drawings on a shared board. The board uses a fixed layout canvas so it looks consistent on different monitors.

Themes

Choose color themes or image themes. Background images, accent colors and icon treatments are applied locally in your browser.

Local backups

Save a ZIP archive with your settings, links, backgrounds and Cloud board content to your computer, and load it later when you need to restore.

Local backups

Backups are optional and start only when you press “Save backup” in Focus settings. The browser opens a save dialog and Focus writes a ZIP archive to the location you choose. The archive contains your Focus settings, links, folders, background images and Cloud board content.

The file is saved on your computer and is not uploaded to the developer, Google or any other server. You can load the ZIP later with “Load backup” to restore your data. The developer has no access to the file.

Privacy and permissions

Focus has no analytics, no advertising and no developer servers. Your links, themes, backgrounds and Cloud board data stay in your browser. The extension uses network access for favicons and search queries you submit. Local backups are saved on your computer. See the Privacy Policy for the full list of permissions and data handling.

Optional access to open tabs is requested only when you choose “Open tabs”; URLs and titles are saved locally and are not sent to the developer.
